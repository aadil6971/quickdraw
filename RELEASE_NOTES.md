QuickDraw is a small native Mac menu-bar app for drawing arrows and boxes over your screen.

- Control–Option–D to toggle drawing
- A for arrows, B for boxes; click and drag to draw
- Shift-drag for squares or arrows snapped to 45°
- Command–Z to undo, Delete to clear, Escape to clear and exit
- Multi-monitor overlays; no accounts or network access

Download the ZIP, extract it, and move QuickDraw.app to Applications. Requires macOS 13+, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

This initial release is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple notarized. macOS may require an explicit Open Anyway approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Source and build instructions are available in the repository.

Validation: universal compilation, geometry self-tests, bundle signature verification, app launch and tool selection. End-to-end mouse dragging and Intel runtime behavior have not been independently verified. Please report problems in Issues.
